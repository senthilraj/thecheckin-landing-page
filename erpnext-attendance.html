What ERPNext and Frappe HRMS do with the data

Once the Employee Checkin records are in ERPNext, your existing ERPNext and Frappe HRMS setup takes over. Nothing new to configure — it plugs into the tools you already have.

ERPNext auto-attendance runs natively

ERPNext's built-in auto-attendance processor reads the Employee Checkin records and generates Attendance documents — Present, Absent, Half Day — based on your existing shift policies.

Frappe HRMS payroll uses the same data

Frappe HRMS reads the Attendance records created by auto-attendance. Leave deductions, LOP calculations, overtime, and shift allowances all process from accurate real-time data.

All ERPNext HR reports work out of the box

Monthly Attendance Sheet, Late Entry report, Early Exit report — they all work because the data is in the standard ERPNext Employee Checkin doctype format.

Secure, signed webhook — no data exposed

Every check-in push is HMAC-signed. No raw face images or biometric data ever leaves TheCheckIn — only the attendance event and the ERPNext employee ID are sent.
