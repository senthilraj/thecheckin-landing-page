How GPS attendance works

Set up once per location. No hardware to install. Location verified every time.

1

Set a geofence for each location

In the admin dashboard, add each work location — job site, office, depot — as a branch. Set the allowed radius (e.g. 200 metres). Staff assigned to that branch must check in from within that zone. Takes 2 minutes per location to configure.

2

Worker arrives and checks in

When the field worker arrives at the site, they open the app and tap Check In. The app reads their GPS location at that moment. If they're within the geofence, the check-in is recorded. If they're outside it, the check-in is blocked or flagged.

3

Location is captured and stored

The exact GPS coordinates at check-in and check-out are stored with the attendance record. You can see where each person was on a map. If a worker's coordinates don't match the expected site, it's visible immediately — not discovered weeks later.

4

Dashboard shows all sites in real time

See which field workers have checked in at each site, their arrival times, and any out-of-zone alerts — all from your phone. At month end, timesheets with hours per site are ready for payroll, broken down by location.

What GPS attendance solves for field teams

No more "I was there" disputes

When a client says your technician arrived late and your technician says they were on time, GPS solves the dispute immediately. The check-in timestamp and coordinates are a permanent record. Neither party's memory is needed — the data is there.

No hardware at each job site

Field teams can't take a biometric machine or a QR code placard to every job site. GPS attendance works anywhere — the phone already in the worker's pocket is the attendance terminal. No infrastructure needed at the site at all.

Multi-site workers tracked per location

A technician who visits 3 sites in one day checks in and out at each one. Their total attendance record shows which site, how long, and what time — broken down per location. Client billing based on hours on-site becomes straightforward.

GPS reads at check-in only — no battery drain

TheCheckIn reads GPS only when a check-in or check-out happens — not continuously. The location read takes less than a second. The app does not run in the background or drain the phone battery during the workday.
