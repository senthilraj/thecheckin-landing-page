How attendance works on a construction site

One-time setup per site. After that, workers check in themselves and you see it in real time.

1

Set up each site as a branch

Add each active site in the admin dashboard — just the site name and GPS coordinates. Set a geofence radius (e.g. 150 meters). Takes 2 minutes per site.

2

Workers register their face once

Each worker opens the Android app, registers their face (30 seconds), and they're set up. Or use QR cards for workers without smartphones.

3

Check in on arrival — GPS confirmed

Workers open the app when they arrive. GPS confirms they're at the right site. Face recognition confirms it's really them. The record hits your dashboard in seconds.

4

Timesheets ready at month end

Daily, weekly, and monthly timesheets are generated automatically per worker and per site. Export to Excel or feed directly into ERPNext or Sage 300 for payroll.

What construction companies get from TheCheckIn

GPS confirms workers are actually on site

The geofence won't accept a check-in from a worker sitting in a car down the road or checking in from home. They have to be physically at the registered site location.

No proxy — face recognition confirms the real worker

A common problem on construction sites: one worker checks in for a colleague who hasn't arrived. The eye-blink liveness test prevents anyone checking in using another person's photo or account.

Track subcontractors separately from permanent staff

Add subcontractors as a separate group, assign them to a specific site, and run their attendance reports independently. Useful when you're billing clients by hours worked or managing multiple sub-vendors on one project.

No hardware to install or maintain at each site

Biometric machines on construction sites get damaged, stolen, or covered in dust. TheCheckIn runs on phones your workers already carry — nothing to mount, power, or repair at the site.
