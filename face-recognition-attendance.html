How face recognition check-in works

Three steps. First time takes 2 minutes. Every check-in after that takes about 5 seconds.

1

Employee registers their face

The employee opens the TheCheckIn app, goes to Face Registration, and looks straight at the camera for about 30 seconds. That's it. No account needed, no email — just their face, once.

2

Check in by looking at the camera

Each day, the employee opens the app and looks at the camera. The app runs a quick liveness check — a blink confirmation — to confirm a real person is present. Not a photo. Not a video. The real employee.

3

Admin sees it instantly

The check-in record hits your admin dashboard in real time — with the employee name, time, and location. At the end of the month, timesheets are already calculated. Export to Excel and hand to payroll.

Why face recognition beats a biometric machine

Zero hardware cost

Biometric terminals cost $200–$800 each and need IT support when they fail. TheCheckIn runs on the phone your employee already owns. No purchase orders, no procurement delays.

Proxy attendance blocked completely

The liveness detection (eye blink test) means a colleague can't check in for someone else using their photo. The real employee has to be physically present, looking at the camera.

Works at any location

No fixed terminal means no fixed check-in point. Field workers, HVAC technicians, and construction teams can check in face-to-camera from any job site. The app records where they were.

Timesheets write themselves

Check-in and check-out times are recorded automatically. Daily, weekly, and monthly timesheets are generated without anyone doing the math. Export to Excel in one click.
