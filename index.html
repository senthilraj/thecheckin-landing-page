QR Types for Employee Attendance

Explore the flexible check-in options supported by TheCheckIn app.

Branch QR

Ideal for office-based attendance

  • Scan QR at the office using your mobile.
  • Location & device verified for security.
  • Prevents spoofing or proxy check-ins.

Employee QR

Works with employee ID cards

  • Printed QR on each employee ID.
  • Scan with office kiosk mobile.
  • Seamless validation using TheCheckIn app.

Direct

No scanning needed

  • Check-in from your mobile directly.
  • GPS location used for validation.
  • Great for remote workers & field teams.
