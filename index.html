Attendance tracking features for every type of team

Whether your team works in an office, on a construction site, or from home — TheCheckIn has a check-in method that fits.

Proxy-proof Primary Feature

Face Recognition Attendance

Employees check in using their face — on any smartphone, no biometric device needed. Liveness detection (eye blink) ensures a real person is present, not a photo. Eliminates buddy punching and proxy attendance completely.

How face check-in works

Branch QR Code Check-in

Place a QR code at each office or site. Employees scan it on their phone when they arrive. No card reader, no biometric machine, no extra hardware — just a printed QR code.

How QR check-in works

GPS Remote Attendance

Field staff and remote workers check in from their location. GPS validation confirms they are at the correct job site — not checking in from a different location.

How GPS attendance works

Employee QR Card

Each employee gets a unique QR card. A manager or shared device scans it for check-in. Ideal for factories, warehouses, and sites where employees share workstations.
