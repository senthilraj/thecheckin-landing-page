Why attendance tracking in Dubai has its own challenges

Dubai businesses often have workforce patterns that standard attendance tools aren't designed for.

Multi-nationality workforce

Teams from 10+ countries, different phone models, different languages. TheCheckIn's Android app is simple enough to use without extensive training — and face registration doesn't depend on language.

Workers at multiple sites

Construction sites, client offices, warehouses across Dubai and the UAE. Each location is a separate branch in TheCheckIn — with its own GPS fence or QR code for check-in.

No reliable terminal at outdoor sites

Biometric terminals need a fixed indoor location and power. A construction site in Meydan or a service call in Sharjah doesn't have that. A phone does.

Payroll accuracy matters

In Dubai, late or incorrect salary payments have regulatory consequences. Accurate attendance data in ERPNext means Frappe HRMS payroll processes correctly every month — no corrections, no disputes.
