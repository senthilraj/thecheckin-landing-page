TheCheckIn is proof of what we build

Before taking a customisation brief, we built our own ERPNext integration from scratch. Here's what that involved — because it tells you what we can build for you.

Face recognition on Android

We built a mobile app with on-device face matching and liveness detection (eye blink), then connected it to ERPNext's HR Attendance module via API. That required custom doctype fields, custom scripts, and careful handling of Frappe's permission model.

GPS-based multi-branch attendance

Field employees check in with GPS. Each branch has a geofence. TheCheckIn validates whether the employee is within range of the registered site before accepting the check-in — and logs the exact coordinates. This feeds into custom branch-level reports in ERPNext.

Automatic timesheet generation

TheCheckIn generates daily, weekly, and monthly timesheets from raw check-in and check-out data. We built the calculation logic, late/early detection, overtime flagging, and an export format that maps cleanly to ERPNext payroll inputs.
