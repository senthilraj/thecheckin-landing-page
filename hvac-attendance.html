What field service companies get from TheCheckIn

GPS confirms they're on site, not around the corner

When a technician checks in with GPS, the app records their exact coordinates. You set the geofence radius for each job site. If they're not close enough, the check-in isn't accepted.

Face recognition confirms it's the actual technician

A colleague can't check in for someone who hasn't arrived yet. The face recognition with liveness detection (eye blink) confirms the real person is there, not just their phone.

Payroll-ready timesheets every month

Total hours, overtime, and attendance status are calculated automatically from check-in and check-out data. Export to Excel and hand to payroll — or import into ERPNext or Sage 300.

Multiple sites, one dashboard

Each client location or recurring job site is a separate branch in TheCheckIn. The admin dashboard shows all technicians across all sites — who's on site, who hasn't checked in, and who's working late.
