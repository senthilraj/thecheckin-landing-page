How branch QR attendance works

Print once, use every day. No maintenance, no hardware to service.

1

Generate and print the QR code

Log in to the admin dashboard, go to your branch, and download the QR code. Print it on A4 or card stock. Laminate it if you want it to last longer. Mount it near the reception or entrance — it's now your permanent check-in point.

2

Staff install the free app once

Each staff member downloads the TheCheckIn app from Google Play — free, one-time setup. They log in once with their registered details. After that, every check-in is just: open app → scan QR. No credentials to type each time.

3

Scan on arrival, scan on exit

When a staff member arrives, they scan the QR. That's their check-in — timestamped, tied to their profile, and logged immediately. When they leave, they scan again for check-out. The total hours for the day are calculated automatically.

4

Dashboard and timesheets update live

The admin dashboard shows who's in right now, who's late, and who hasn't arrived. At month end, total hours per person are ready to export. No reconciling — it's all already there from the daily scans.

Why branch QR check-in works better than a paper register

The timestamp can't be changed after the fact

A paper register can be filled in at the end of the day with a made-up time. A QR scan records the exact moment it was scanned — and that record is permanent. Staff can see their own history; only admins can make corrections, and corrections are logged.

Each QR is unique to one location

If you have 3 branches, each has its own QR code. A staff member checking in at Branch A using Branch A's QR is recorded as present at Branch A — not a generic "present." Multi-location attendance is separated automatically without any admin work.

3 seconds per person — faster than signing a register

Signing a register takes longer than a QR scan, especially if the pen isn't there or the register is across the room. QR scanning is faster, requires no writing, and the dashboard update is instant. For teams that rush through the door at 9:00am, it creates zero friction.

Add face recognition for proxy prevention

QR check-in alone confirms the phone was there — not who was holding it. If proxy attendance is a concern, enable face recognition alongside QR. The staff member scans the QR and then completes a face match. Both checks pass before the attendance is recorded.

QR vs other check-in methods

Branch QR (this page)

Best for: offices, retail, restaurants, classrooms. Staff arrive at a fixed location and scan on entry. Simple and fast.

Face Recognition

Best for: proxy prevention, high-security sites, clinics. Verifies identity — not just that a phone is present.

GPS Attendance

Best for: field workers, construction sites, delivery drivers. Confirms location when there's no fixed QR point.

Employee QR Card

Best for: factory workers without smartphones. Supervisor scans each worker's printed ID card.
