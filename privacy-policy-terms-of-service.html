Privacy Policy

Introduction

TheCheckIn application is developed and maintained by Pazhanam Technologies Private Limited, a company recognized by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Government of India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our attendance and time tracking application.

Information We Collect

To provide our services effectively, we collect the following information:

  • Company Registration Details: Business name, registration number, tax information, and other legal details required for verification.
  • Employee Details: First name, last name, and email address (mandatory); mobile number and date of birth (optional).
  • Office/Branch Address: Physical locations where employees check in/out.
  • GPS Location: Precise location data during check-in and check-out for attendance validation.

How We Use Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Validating employee attendance through location verification
  • Generating accurate timesheets and attendance reports
  • Improving our services and developing new features
  • Providing customer support and troubleshooting

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to outside parties. Your information is used solely for the purpose of providing and improving TheCheckIn service.

Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information:

  • All passwords are encrypted using industry-standard protocols
  • Data is stored securely in cloud servers (such as AWS) with restricted access
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Compliance with applicable data protection regulations

Communication

We may contact the company administrator via the registered email address for important updates, service notifications, feedback requests, or information about upgrades and new features. These communications are considered part of the service and you may not opt out of them while maintaining an active account.

Account Deletion

Company administrators can delete their account at any time through the Help page in the application. Upon confirmation of account deletion, all associated data including company information, employee records, and attendance history will be permanently erased from our systems immediately. This action cannot be undone.
