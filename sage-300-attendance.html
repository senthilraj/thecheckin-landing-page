How attendance data moves from TheCheckIn to Sage 300

A straightforward flow — face check-in on the phone, timesheet in Sage 300 at the end of the period.

1

Employee checks in on their phone

Face recognition with liveness detection on the Android app. Check-in time, check-out time, and working hours are recorded automatically in TheCheckIn's dashboard. GPS also logs the location if you need it for remote or multi-site teams.

2

Export timesheet at pay period end

From the admin dashboard, export the period's attendance as Excel or CSV. The export includes employee ID, date, in-time, out-time, total hours, and attendance status — formatted to match Sage 300's import layout.

3

Import into Sage 300 T&A

Import the file into Sage 300's Time and Attendance module. From there, payroll processes normally — Sage 300 calculates pay based on actual hours worked, overtime, and any deductions. No manual entry, no corrections at month end.

Why companies replace their biometric clock with TheCheckIn

Biometric terminals break and cost $400+ to replace

TheCheckIn runs on phones employees already own. If someone's phone breaks, they get a new phone. No replacement terminal, no vendor support contract.

One terminal means a queue at 9am

Every employee checks in separately on their own phone — no bottleneck at the door. Face recognition takes about 5 seconds. No one is late because the terminal was backed up.

Fingerprint readers fail for workers with worn hands

Warehouse staff, cleaners, and manual workers often have fingerprints that don't read well — especially toward end of shift. Face recognition works regardless of what their hands look like.

Works for remote and field teams too

A fixed biometric terminal can't track workers at satellite offices, warehouses, or client sites. TheCheckIn's GPS check-in covers remote employees too — all in the same dashboard and Sage 300 export.
