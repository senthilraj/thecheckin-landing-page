How restaurant shift attendance works with TheCheckIn

No hardware. No paper. Every shift gets a clean digital record automatically.

1

Set up shifts and branches

Create your AM and PM shifts in the admin dashboard. Add each restaurant location as a branch. Takes 5 minutes and you don't touch it again unless your schedule changes.

2

Staff check in on arrival

When the chef arrives, they scan the kitchen QR code or check in with face recognition. 3 seconds. No app login every time — just scan and go. Latecomers are automatically flagged.

3

Manager sees headcount live

Before service starts you can check — is the full kitchen team in? Did the evening crew arrive? See it from your phone without calling anyone. Know if you're going to be short-staffed before service opens.

4

Pay period closes automatically

At the end of the month, total hours per staff member are calculated from check-in and check-out data. Export to Excel for payroll — or connect to ERPNext or Sage 300 for direct import.

What restaurant owners get from TheCheckIn

The paper register is gone — no one misses it

Paper registers get left blank during busy service, filled in retroactively, or just lost. A QR code on the wall doesn't get lost. Every scan is a permanent, timestamped record that nobody can alter.

No more proxy check-ins between kitchen and FOH

In a busy restaurant, it's easy for one staff member to mark another as present. Face recognition with liveness detection stops that completely — only the actual person can check in for themselves.

Late arrival alerts before service starts

You can see who hasn't checked in yet as the shift start time approaches. If a key staff member is running late, you know in time to call them or arrange cover — not 20 minutes after service should have opened.

Payroll disputes end when every hour is documented

When a part-time staff member says they worked 18 hours last week and your calculation says 14, TheCheckIn shows the exact check-in and check-out timestamps. No dispute — just the record.
