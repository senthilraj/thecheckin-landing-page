TheCheckIn replaces the machine with an app. Workers check in by scanning their employee QR card — a printed card, no smartphone required on their side. Or use face recognition on their own phone if they have one. Either way, every check-in is timestamped and goes straight to the dashboard.

How factory attendance works with TheCheckIn

Works for daily-wage workers, contract labour, and permanent staff — no enrollment machines, no IT setup.

1

Add workers from the admin dashboard

Enter each worker's name and phone number — that's all. No fingerprint enrollment, no biometric setup. New contract workers can be added in 30 seconds, before they walk through the gate.

2

Print employee QR cards

Each worker gets a printed QR card with their unique code. Print them yourself — any printer works. Workers carry the card like an ID badge. No app installation needed on the worker's side.

3

Supervisor scans cards at gate entry

As workers arrive for their shift, the gate supervisor scans each card with one phone. Takes 2 seconds per person. Or set up a shared device at the entry point so workers scan themselves as they walk in.

4

Timesheets calculate automatically

Hours worked are calculated from check-in and check-out times. Daily wage workers, weekly contract staff, and permanent employees all tracked the same way. Export to Excel for payroll — or connect to ERPNext.

Why factories switch from biometric machines to TheCheckIn

No machine to maintain or replace

Biometric machines in factory environments fail — dust, humidity, oil on hands all affect fingerprint readers. When the machine breaks, attendance tracking stops until a technician comes. With TheCheckIn, the device is a phone. If it breaks, use another one.

Add contract workers the same day they arrive

Biometric enrollment takes time, a technician, and the machine has to be working. TheCheckIn adds a worker in under a minute. For factories with daily or weekly contract labour, this alone saves hours of admin per month.

Proxy check-in stopped at the gate

Workers sharing QR cards or checking in for absent colleagues is caught immediately — each QR card is unique and one check-in per shift per person is enforced. Enable face recognition for higher-security environments where proxy is a bigger problem.

Payroll-ready data, not raw punch logs

The dashboard shows total hours per worker, broken down by day and shift. One-click Excel export with columns formatted for payroll input. No re-entering data from printed punch slips, no transcription errors.
